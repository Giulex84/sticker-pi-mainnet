Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Sticker.pi uses Pi SDK authentication and processes only the information needed to operate the app, including the app-specific Pi user ID and username returned after user authorization.

Authoritative gameplay data may include level, XP, daily streak, sticker collection, pack inventory, duplicates, challenge statistics, limited anti-abuse timestamps and verified purchase identifiers. A non-authoritative display cache may also be stored on the user's device.

Sticker.pi records privacy-preserving aggregate usage counts, such as daily active users, logins, challenge activity, pack openings, album completion and completed purchases. User identifiers used for daily counting are pseudonymized server-side and are not displayed in analytics.

Sticker.pi never asks for, stores or needs a Pi Wallet passphrase, private key or seed phrase.

Optional User-to-App payments use Pi Network's official SDK and APIs. The backend verifies supported transactions before granting the displayed in-app item. Vercel and Upstash may process minimal technical data required to host the service and persistent database.

Payment identifiers are retained as needed to prevent duplicate fulfillment and resolve transaction issues. Gameplay and aggregate telemetry data are retained while required to provide, secure and improve the service and may be removed when the service or account data is retired, subject to security and transaction-record requirements.

Peer-to-peer exchange and A2U rewards are not active in this release.
