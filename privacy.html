Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Sticker.pi uses Pi SDK authentication and processes only information needed to operate the app, including your app-specific Pi user ID and username.

Authoritative gameplay data is stored server-side and may include level, XP, daily streak, sticker collection, pack inventory, duplicates, challenge statistics, limited anti-abuse timestamps and verified purchase identifiers. A non-authoritative display cache may also be stored on your device.

Sticker.pi never asks for, stores or needs your Pi Wallet passphrase, private key or seed phrase.

Payment processing uses Pi Network's official SDK and APIs. Supported Pi transactions are verified server-side before a purchased pack is granted. Service providers such as Vercel and Upstash may process minimal technical request data required to host the app and database.

Gameplay balances, seasonal content and non-purchased rewards may change as the service evolves. Payment records are retained as needed to prevent duplicate grants and resolve transaction issues.
